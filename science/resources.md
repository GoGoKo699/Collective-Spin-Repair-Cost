# Resources and operational scope

[Model](model.md) · [Converse](converse.md) · [Construction](construction.md)

The theorem prices microscopic operations that change total-spin sectors while allowing a deliberately generous class of Casimir-preserving controls. Keeping those two resources distinct makes the converse stronger and makes its implementation limits explicit.

## Charged repair

For the supplied local decomposition of the symmetry-breaking generator, the pointwise budget is

```math
B(t)=\sum_X|X|\left(\|h_X(t)\|+
                  \sum_\mu\|L_{X\mu}(t)\|^2\right)\le B,
\qquad |X|\le k.
```

The locality order $`k`$ is fixed as $`N`$ grows. Hamiltonian norms and squared jump norms both have units of inverse time, with $`\hbar=1`$. The support weight counts the physical noisy spins touched by a term. A protected controller or ancillary system can be included, but its coupling to physical spins is charged by the same support and norm rule unless that individual term commutes with the physical Casimir.

The budget belongs to the actual supplied decomposition. No invariance under Lindblad gauge changes, optimization over alternative decompositions, or identification with dissipated heat or optical power is asserted. The hypothesis is a hard instantaneous cap; for adaptive implementations it must hold along every allowed control branch. A time average or an expected expenditure is a different resource contract.

If repair touches at most $`r`$ spins with a uniformly bounded total microscopic strength/rate $`\Gamma_{\rm site}`$ per addressed spin, the specified decomposition gives $`B=O(r\Gamma_{\rm site})`$. The converse then requires $`r\Gamma_{\rm site}=\Omega(\kappa N)`$ for a fixed Heisenberg-order collective phase-QFI. This implication does not count the separate cost of the free collective controls.

## Free controls are supplied resources

Every free Hamiltonian and every individual free jump commutes with

```math
C=S_x^2+S_y^2+S_z^2.
```

Their strength, nonlinearity, spatial range, classical description and synthesis cost are unrestricted in the theorem. This class contains ordinary collective spin rotations and nonlinear collective operations, and is larger than a prescribed cavity or one-axis-twisting control menu. It also allows entropy-removing operations within each spin sector. Merely being permutation symmetric is not the definition used in the proof; the commutator conditions are the actual hypotheses.

The upper bound remains valid in this generous class. The matching construction uses it. Therefore the result is not an end-to-end resource optimum for a physical apparatus with bounded collective interactions.

## Counted matching construction

Let $`b=B/\kappa`$. For $`1\le b\le N`$ the protocol in [Construction](construction.md) uses:

| Resource | Explicit use |
|---|---|
| Physical noisy spins | $`N`$ |
| Initial state | $`I/2^N`$ |
| Locally pumped spins | $`m=\lfloor b\rfloor`$ |
| Jump on each pumped spin | $`\sqrt\kappa\,\lvert0\rangle\langle1\rvert`$ |
| Instantaneous charged repair | $`m\kappa\le B`$ during pumping; zero during the final pulse |
| Pump duration | $`s=T-\tau\ge\kappa^{-1}`$ |
| Final collective Hamiltonian | $`H_{\rm pulse}=\Omega X^{\otimes N}`$ |
| Final pulse duration | $`\tau=\pi/(4\Omega)`$ |
| Sufficient pulse strength | $`\Omega\ge 2\pi\kappa N^3/[p_*^4(N+m^2)]`$, with $`p_*=(1-e^{-2})/2`$ |
| Noise during preparation | Independent depolarization at rate $`\kappa`$ throughout pumping and pulse |
| Additional quantum ancilla in this construction | None |
| Phase imprint | Applied only after preparation, generated by $`S_z`$ |

For prescribed $`T\ge\kappa^{-1}\log(N+2)`$ these choices fit into the same deterministic time horizon used for the uniform late-time statement. The ideal pulse gives $`F_Q\ge p_*^4(N+m^2)`$, and the finite noisy pulse retains at least $`9/20`$ of that value by a fixed-measurement witness. The resulting order matches $`\min\lbrace N^2,N+b^2\rbrace `$ in the stated range. For $`B\ge\kappa N`$, set $`m=N`$ and keep the same construction.

The displayed $`\Omega`$ ranges from a sufficient order $`\kappa N^2`$ near the SQL regime to order $`\kappa N`$ when $`m`$ is proportional to $`N`$. These are conservative **sufficient** norm scalings for this direct many-body pulse, not necessary collective-strength bounds. An implementation through one-axis twisting or another interaction must charge that implementation's own norm, elapsed time, precision and compilation overhead. No polynomial elementary-gate compiler for the whole preparation is supplied by the repair theorem.

The no-repair SQL baseline uses the explicitly specified global spin-sector reset generator at a sufficient rate $`64\kappa N^2`$. Its jumps commute with $`C`$, but are highly engineered and not local reservoirs. It establishes an order-$`N`$ floor under the full supplied free class, without claiming a low-cost physical realization.

## Why the time and output conventions matter

The finite-time converse retains the actual initial-spin resource through a term of order

```math
e^{-\kappa T}\mathrm{Tr}(\rho(0)C).
```

Since the initial moment can be order $`N^2`$, a fixed preparation time of order $`\kappa^{-1}`$ does not uniformly erase its Heisenberg-order contribution. A late-time limit, or the explicit logarithmic-in-$`N`$ horizon, is part of the claim. The exact no-repair drift has the sharper decay factor $`e^{-2\kappa T}`$; the repair inequality uses a convenient weaker exponent after Young's inequality.

The output time is prescribed, rather than selected by uncharged postselection or a stopping rule that waits for a rare high-spin branch. If preparation outcomes are retained, they are parameter independent; a weighted conditional-QFI statement uses their actual probabilities. The terminal state or record-assisted phase-QFI is not the Fisher information accumulated during an entire parameter-dependent sensing experiment.

For every physical $`N`$-spin state with any protected ancilla, the later collective signal generator $`G=\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf S\otimes I_A`$ has spectrum in $`[-N/2,N/2]`$. Therefore

```math
F_Q(\rho_{SA},G)\le4\mathrm{Var}_{\rho_{SA}}G\le N^2.
```

Protected ancillas do not enlarge that generator's spectral range. The moment proof also bounds their assistance through the physical reduced-state Casimir. This does not say that protected ancillas or their physical implementation are free in a laboratory; it says that even granting them does not evade the stated repair converse.

## Scientific meaning and exclusions

The operational result is a resource law for preparing collective probes after persistent independent noise has depleted the initial spin-sector resource. Above the SQL floor, repair throughput of order greater than $`\kappa\sqrt N`$ is needed for an asymptotically growing collective phase gain; order $`\kappa N`$ is needed for $`\Theta(N^2)`$ phase-QFI. Unlimited Casimir-preserving processing cannot replace this microscopic sector-changing throughput.

The law is not a thermodynamic-work bound, a general error-correction threshold, a statement about one-axis dephasing, a differential-signal bound from the global Casimir alone, or a protected-frequency-sensing advantage. Noise during phase encoding, geometry, bounded collective bandwidth, monitoring and memory hardware introduce different questions.
