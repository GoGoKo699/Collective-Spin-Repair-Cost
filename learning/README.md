# Learning route

The single educational anchor is **[Heinz-Peter Breuer and Francesco Petruccione, *The Theory of Open Quantum Systems* (2002)](https://academic.oup.com/book/27757)**.

The route starts from density operators and composite systems in Chapter 2, then uses Chapter 3, Sections **3.2.1–3.2.3**, for quantum dynamical semigroups, the quantum Markov master equation, and the adjoint master equation. These are broad section pointers; the project does not require the whole book as a prerequisite.

Read the [source-to-project bridge](BRIDGE.md) for the notation translation, the physical meaning of the repair budget, two small complete examples, and the steps supplied by this research. The [30-milestone map](MILESTONES.md) records the project's understanding targets and their dependencies. It is a common scientific map, independent of any reader's study history.

For a direct technical route, use the [scientific guide](../science/README.md): [model](../science/model.md), [converse](../science/converse.md), [construction](../science/construction.md), and [resources](../science/resources.md). The [verification guide](../verification/README.md) explains which finite calculations can be reproduced and what they establish.

The book supplies open-system foundations. QFI, the spin-sector repair inequality, and the matching preparation are developed in the project. The [research bibliography](../references/references.bib) and [related-work comparison](../references/RELATED_WORK.md) credit the additional primary literature; they are not additional educational anchors.
